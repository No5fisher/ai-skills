> 标注约定：【直接引用】= 有记录原话 | 【推断】= 基于多处来源综合判断 | 【存疑】= 需核实

## 一、Berkshire Hathaway 年度股东大会（1994年起完整录像）

「奥马哈朝圣」——每年5月第一个周末，数万人聚集奥马哈，Buffett 和 Munger 连续回答问题6小时以上。这是理解他「即兴思考方式」最重要的来源。

### 标志性问答模式

**当被问到具体股票时**：
> "I can't comment on individual securities. But I can tell you what I look for in a business…"
——然后用通用框架绕过推荐，但实质上分享了他真实的评估逻辑。

**当被问到市场走向时（每年必问）**：
> "I don't know what the stock market will do tomorrow, next month, or next year. I never have known, and I never will know. Nobody does. The question is whether businesses will be worth more in 10-20 years. I think the answer is clearly yes."

**2008年股东大会（金融危机中）**：
> "We're in a financial Pearl Harbor. It's been an economic tsunami. But we've been through things like this before. America has faced much worse situations and still came through. I've invested in America all my life, and I'll continue to."

**2020年股东大会（COVID疫情，首次线上举行）**：
> "I'm 89 years old. I've seen a lot. I don't know what's going to happen next week, next month. But I do know what America has produced over the centuries, and it's been remarkable."
——独自主持，无 Munger（因健康原因未出席）。

**2023年股东大会（Charlie Munger 去世前最后一次同台）**：
Munger 最后一次公开亮相，两人的互动成为传记式历史记录。

**2024年股东大会（Munger 去世后首次）**：
> "Charlie is here in spirit. I miss him every day. He made me a much better investor."
明显的情感流露，不同于平时的幽默基调。

### 股东大会的语言特征
- 对最刁钻的技术性问题，往往用「我不会那么框架化地思考这个问题」重新定向
- 对愚蠢的问题，温和幽默地处理而不羞辱提问者
- 对批评，正面承认（「那是个好观点，我以前没这么想过」）

## 二、CNBC「投资者精神」系列访谈（Becky Quick，长期合作）

Buffett 最频繁接受的媒体访谈形式，Becky Quick 是他最信任的记者之一。

【直接引用，关于时机】：
> "The stock market is a no-called-strike game. You don't have to swing at everything — you can wait for your pitch."

【直接引用，关于错误】：
> "I've made every mistake in the book. The amazing thing is I'm still here. Surviving is the prerequisite to playing."

【直接引用，关于 AI（2023年最新立场）】：
> "I don't understand it. That means I should stay away from investing in it. That doesn't mean it won't be important — it probably will be very important. It just means I can't evaluate it."（存疑：具体措辞需核实）

## 三、大学演讲（哥伦比亚大学、内布拉斯加大学等）

Buffett 是哥伦比亚大学商学院的最著名校友（Ben Graham 的学生），多次回校演讲。

**哥伦比亚大学（1984年，「格雷厄姆-多德的超级投资者们」）**：
这是他最重要的学术演讲之一，驳斥「股市有效」假说。

【直接引用】：
> "I would like you to imagine a national coin-flipping contest. Let's say we get 225 million Americans up tomorrow morning and we ask them all to wager a dollar. They go out in the morning at sunrise, and they all call the flip of a coin... After ten flips on ten mornings, there will be approximately 220,000 people in the United States who have correctly called ten flips in a row... Now this group will have been getting progressively puffier. Some of them will write books on 'How I turned a Dollar into a Million in Ten Days Working Thirty Seconds a Morning'... If we find that 40 of the 220,000 winners came from a particular zoo in Omaha, we'd be pretty sure we were on to something."

这是「价值投资者来自同一个智识家族（Ben Graham）」的著名比喻。

**对学生的核心建议（贯穿多次演讲）**：
> "The most important investment you can make is in yourself. It will be worth 10 times anything else you do."

> "Don't watch the market. Evaluate businesses. The market is there to serve you, not to instruct you."

## 四、TED 与其他公共演讲（较少）

Buffett 相对少参加 TED 类活动，更偏好股东大会和大学演讲。

**最知名的公共时刻：2011年 NYT 专栏「Stop Coddling the Super-Rich」**
这是他最具政治争议性的文章——呼吁对富人征收更高税率，当时他的有效税率（17.4%）比他的秘书（36%）还低。

【直接引用】：
> "My friends and I have been coddled long enough by a billionaire-friendly Congress. It's time for our government to get serious about shared sacrifice."

这篇文章在财富圈引发强烈反应，Charles Koch 等人直接反驳他「可以自愿多缴税」。

## 五、与 Charlie Munger 的互动记录

Munger 是 Buffett 公开表达中最常引用的人。他们60年的合伙关系产生了大量公开对话记录。

**Buffett 对 Munger 的总结性描述**：
> "Charlie has the best 30-second mind in the world. He sees to the kernel of every problem."

> "He corrected me from buying cheap businesses to buying great businesses at fair prices. That's been worth billions of dollars to Berkshire."

**Munger 从外部描述 Buffett 的核心特质**：
> "His willingness to sit there and do nothing is extraordinary. Most people can't stand that. Warren can." （出自 Poor Charlie's Almanack）

**Munger 离世后（2023年11月28日，99岁）**，Buffett 的公开表达：
> "Berkshire Hathaway could not have been built to its present status without Charlie's inspiration, wisdom and participation." —— 官方声明

> "I think I'm talking to him all the time. He's still in my life." ——非正式场合

## 六、被追问时的标准应对模式

【推断，基于大量访谈的一致模式】

**被问到「为什么没买 X」时**：
不说「那是个错误」，而说「它不在我的能力圈里」或「我没法对它10年后的竞争地位有足够把握」。

**被问到「下一次危机是什么」时**：
> "I don't know, and anyone who says they do know is wrong. What I do know is that opportunities come from panic."

**被问到政治话题时**：
谨慎但不回避——他在民主党方向的政治立场相对公开（支持奥巴马、希拉里），但不把政治观点带入投资建议。

**被问到竞争对手时**（基金经理、PE）：
> "I've never understood why people complain about competition. If you're really good at something, you should welcome the chance to prove it."

**面对批评时（如「你错过了技术行业」）**：
模式：承认事实 → 解释逻辑 → 不辩护但不道歉。
> "Yes, I missed Amazon. Yes, I missed Google. I knew both were brilliant businesses. I just didn't know how to assess where they would be in 10-20 years. That's the honest truth."

## 七、晚年语气变化（2020年后）

与早年相比，明显变化：
1. **更多情感表达**：Munger 去世后，他在公开场合更愿意表达依恋和失去
2. **更明确的接班叙事**：从2020年起，大量讨论以「Greg Abel 会怎么做」而非「我会怎么做」
3. **对 AI 的坦诚困惑**：承认不理解，而非忽视
4. **对错误更坦然**：Kraft Heinz 的承认在他早年几乎不会这么直接
